Connect your private AI

  1. Open NVIDIA’s model catalog, sign in, and generate an API key for a model available to your account.
  2. Paste the key below, enter the model identifier from the catalog, set a daily limit, and enable private AI.
  3. Save your settings, then test the connection. The test uses one model call and may incur provider charges.

Calls are limited to 2,048 output tokens, one at a time. Failed calls count toward the daily limit. Costs shown are your estimates, not provider billing; retries can add charges. Check your provider's pricing and account usage.
